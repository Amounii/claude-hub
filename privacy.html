Claude Hub: privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026.

Claude Hub is a personal tool used only by its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer and accesses only the owner's own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads his Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, to help him with his own email, schedule and files.

What it does with the data

The data is processed on the owner's computer and by the AI assistant he uses (Claude, by Anthropic), only to carry out his own requests. It is not shared with anyone else, not sold, and not used for advertising.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page "Third-party apps & services".
